Common warning signs

⏰ It rushes you

"Within 24 hours", "final notice", "today only". Scammers want you to act before you think.

🔑 It asks for secrets

A real bank or shop never asks for your password, PIN or full card details in a message.

✉️ The sender looks slightly wrong

Misspelt names (digibnak), odd domains, or a number you do not recognise. Check the address, not the display name.

🔗 A link you did not expect

Do not click it. Go to the site yourself, the way you normally do, and check there.

💸 Pay first, get paid later

Fees to release a prize or refund, "safe accounts", and requests from "a friend with a new number" are classic traps.

🧾 A bill you do not recognise

If you never signed up with the biller, or the amount has no meter reading behind it, do not pay it.
