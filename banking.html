How Banking Works

A two-minute guide to the accounts and tools in DigiBank.

Checking vs. savings
Checking is your everyday account: transfers, card and loan payments, and savings-goal contributions all come out of it. Savings is money you set aside; in a real bank it usually earns interest and may limit withdrawals. Use Move Money to shift funds between the two — it's free and doesn't change your total.
Transfers
A transfer sends money to someone else's account and can't be pulled back, so always double-check the name and account number. Scheduled transfers repeat automatically — if the account is short on funds when one is due, it's skipped and you get a message.
Credit card utilization
Utilization is how much of your credit limit you're using (balance ÷ limit). Keeping it under 30% helps your credit score; paying the full balance each month avoids interest entirely.
Loan interest
A loan's APR is the yearly cost of borrowing. Longer terms mean smaller monthly payments but more total interest — compare the "Total to Repay" figure, not just the monthly payment.
Savings goals
Naming a goal and a target makes saving concrete. Small regular contributions add up; watching the progress bar is a proven way to stay on track.
Alerts
Low-balance and large-transaction alerts arrive in your Messages inbox — a simple habit that helps you catch mistakes and fraud early.
